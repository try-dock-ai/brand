# Dock Brand Use Policy

The marks, wordmarks, and visual elements in this repo (the "Dock Brand") are owned by **Vector Apps, Inc.** This document describes what you can and can't do with them. It's intentionally short and human-readable.

## What you can do without asking

- Use the **mark** or **lockup** in articles, blog posts, podcast cover art, conference talk slides, integration partner pages, and similar editorial / educational contexts where you're genuinely talking about Dock.
- Hot-link the assets directly from `raw.githubusercontent.com/try-dock-ai/brand/main/...` — that's why this repo is public.
- Include the brand colors and typography in mockups, design explorations, or compatibility documentation.
- Show the mark or lockup as part of a list of integrations, tools, or services your product works with.
- Make it clear in writing or markup that you're referring to "Dock" (or "trydock.ai") so users know what the brand represents.

## What needs our okay first

Email <support@trydock.ai>:

- Merch (t-shirts, stickers, mugs) — even free giveaways.
- Event sponsorship branding, booth materials, large-format prints.
- Domain names or app store listings that include "Dock" or use the mark as an icon.
- Any commercial product whose primary value is reproducing the brand.

## What you can't do

- Modify the mark or wordmark — no recoloring, stretching, adding strokes, embedding in other shapes, animating, or remixing.
- Use the brand to imply Vector Apps endorses, sponsors, or is partnered with you when we aren't.
- Use the brand on anything misleading, harmful, illegal, deceptive, or against our [Terms](https://trydock.ai/terms).
- Use the brand to confuse users into thinking your product is Dock (no logo on a competing product, no Dock-named copycat, etc.).

## Trust marks

Building a Dock integration and want to show "Works with Dock"? Use the mark + the words "Works with Dock" in plain type below it. Don't combine the mark with your own product name into a single composite logo.

- The README, USAGE, and any text/code in this repo are MIT-licensed — fork and adapt freely for your own brand-kit repos.

If you're not sure whether a use is okay: ask first. We're friendly.
